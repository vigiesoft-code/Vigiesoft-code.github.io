S&P 500 futures & options

Real-time market analytics, built on our own data feed.

What Sentinelle does

One platform that goes from raw market data to a chart a trader can act on, without depending on third-party analytics.

Live data capture

Continuous ingestion of real-time futures and options market data for S&P 500 instruments, including ES, SPX and SPY.

Server-side analytics

Data is stored, aggregated into candles and analysed on the server on a short, fixed processing cycle.

Key price levels

The platform derives price levels of interest from options activity and market structure, and tracks how price reacts to them.

Chart-first delivery

Levels and signals are displayed as overlays on live price charts, so the analysis is read where trading decisions are made.

How it works

Sentinelle runs on its own data feed, from the exchange data subscription to the browser.

01 — CAPTURE

Market data in

Licensed real-time feeds are captured by dedicated services and written to the database as they arrive.

02 — PROCESS

Aggregate and compute

SQL Server aggregates ticks into multi-timeframe candles and computes levels and signals through stored procedures.

03 — SERVE

Datafeed API

An ASP.NET Core backend exposes history over REST and live updates over WebSocket to the web client.

04 — DISPLAY

Interactive charts

The web client renders live candles together with Sentinelle's computed levels and signal annotations.
