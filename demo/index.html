The indicators

What each indicator shows and how it is drawn. They follow the chart's timeframe, including the overnight session when data exists, and refresh 10 seconds after each bar closes: updates are driven by the market, never by a timer.

Options POI — option walls, summed (on price)

Shows where options on three markets (options on ES futures, options on the S&P 500 index, options on the S&P 500 ETF) concentrate their positions, brought to the ES price scale and summed when they land on the same price.

  • Types: Magnet (open-interest magnet), OI Support / OI Resistance, Flow Wall, Absorption.
  • Strength: one scale for all three sources: weak, medium, strong.

Options Zones Accumulation — the boxes (on price)

Identifies periods when ES price holds a narrow band while options accumulate flow.

  • One rectangle per zone; the Σ label is the cumulative options aggressor flow since the zone opened; an arrow marks the exit, up or down.

Options Legs — the fuel of each leg (on price)

Splits price into legs and measures the options aggressor flow during each one.

  • A Σ label at the pivot that ends the leg; the current leg is dotted.

Options Aggressor — flow, bar by bar (panel)

Net options aggressor flow for the selected participant: consolidated Σ (default), SPY options, SPX options or ES options.

  • Histogram of the bar's flow, a stock line (cumulated since the last leg pivot, or since the zone opened), its moving average, and "stock zero-retest" points on Σ (a reference, not validated).

Options vs Price — did price follow the flow? (panel)

Compares options aggressor flow with what ES price did over the same window.

  • Healthy: strong flow and price moves in its direction.
  • Absorption: strong flow and price does not move, or moves against it.
  • Vacuum: price moves without flow (the order book is thinning).
  • Neutral: everything else.
  • Window: the rolling minute on 15 s and 30 s charts; the full bar on 1 min and 5 min charts, with thresholds tuned per timeframe.

Options Entry — the flow oscillator (panel)

An entry oscillator applied to the cumulative Σ options flow instead of price.

  • Price Line (fast), Liquid Eq (slow), equilibrium line at 50. Touch points are visual markers only, not validated.

Design conventions

Shared by the whole indicator family.

ElementConvention
BackgroundDark theme; context objects are translucent, markers stay in the foreground.
CyanWith the flow: price follows the flow, the flow goes with the leg, zone exit on the side of the stock.
OrangeAgainst or blocked: absorption, flow against the leg, exit against the stock.
VioletVacuum (price slides without flow); Magnet for POI.
GreyNeutral; Absorption for POI (it has no side).
Green / redBuy / sell (flow); support / resistance (POI).
DottedIn progress: a zone, leg or segment still alive, extending with new bars.
Line weightStrength (weak, medium, strong) for POI.
LabelsΣ first; the breakdown by source (SPY · SPX · ES) is optional.
LegendsOn the right of each panel.

Data sources

MarketWhat is read
E-mini S&P 500 futures (ES)Reference price (zones, legs, price reaction, POI scale).
Options on ES futuresQuotes and trades per contract, buy and sell volume, open interest.
Options on the S&P 500 index (SPX)Trades and quotes, open interest, short-dated expiries.
Options on the S&P 500 ETF (SPY)Same data as SPX.
Underlyings (index and ETF)Spot prices.

Derived from these sources

Aggressor side. Each option trade is classified as a buy or a sell depending on whether it hits the seller or the buyer; calls and puts are signed to give a net bullish or bearish flow.

Consolidated Σ. The three options markets brought to a common unit and summed (internal formula, under validation). It is an aggregate: disagreement between participants cancels out in it.
